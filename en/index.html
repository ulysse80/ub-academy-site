FAQ

Your questions, my answers

Who are the lessons for?

High-school students, university students, adults and professionals, from beginner to advanced. I adapt the lessons to your profile, your pace and your goal: studies, an exam, work or everyday communication.

Which languages can I learn?

French, English and Mandarin Chinese, one-on-one or in a group, depending on your level and your goals.

Which international exams do you prepare for?

TCF, TEF, DELF and DALF for French; IELTS, TOEFL and Cambridge exams for English; HSK for Chinese. We work on exam formats and strategies, with targeted practice and mock tests.

Do you offer one-on-one lessons, group lessons and coaching?

Yes. You can choose one-on-one lessons, group lessons, or professional communication coaching: job interviews, presentations and public speaking.

Are the lessons fully online?

Yes, all my lessons and coaching sessions are 100% online, live by video call. You can join from Morocco or anywhere else in the world: we simply agree on a suitable time.

Are the lessons personalized?

Yes. I adjust the content, exercises and pace to your level and priorities, and we review your progress regularly to adapt the program as you go.

How do I sign up?

Message me on WhatsApp or use the form. In a free 15-minute first chat, we go over your level, your goal, the format you prefer and your availability. I then send you a clear, personalized proposal.

How quickly will I progress?

It depends on your starting level, your goal, how often we meet and your own practice. I don't promise everyone the same result: after the initial assessment, I give you a realistic estimate and regular milestones to measure your progress.

How long is a session?

A session usually lasts 1 hour. Depending on your needs and goals, I can also offer 1.5-hour sessions.

How many people are in a group?

Group size depends on the program and format. I'll tell you how your group is made up during our first chat.

Which payment methods do you accept?

I share the payment details together with your personalized proposal, before you sign up.

What happens if I miss a session?

To keep things fair for everyone, a confirmed session that you don't attend counts as taken: it is neither refunded nor rescheduled. If something comes up, please let me know as early as possible.
