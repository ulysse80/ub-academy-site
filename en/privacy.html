Privacy policy

This page explains what information is collected when you contact me through this website, what it is used for and what your rights are.

Who is responsible for your data?

Ulysse Bordereau, founder of UB Academy. For any question: ubacademyofficial@gmail.com.

What information is collected?

  • Through the contact form: your name, phone number, email address (optional), subject and message.
  • Through WhatsApp: your number and the messages you send me.

Nothing else: there is no account to create and no payment on this website.

What is it used for?

Only to reply to your request, discuss your goal and send you a lesson proposal. It is never sold, rented or shared.

Who has access to it?

Only me. To work, the website relies on a few technical services:

  • Web3Forms: sends the form by email;
  • Gmail (Google): receives those emails;
  • WhatsApp (Meta): if you message me on WhatsApp;
  • GitHub: hosts the website;
  • Google Fonts: provides the website's fonts.

These services may process technical data, such as your IP address, under their own privacy policies.

How long is it kept?

As long as needed to handle your request and follow up on your lessons, and no more than 3 years after our last exchange. It is then deleted.

Cookies

This website sets no cookies and uses no tracking or analytics tools.

Your rights

You can ask at any time to access, correct or delete your information, or object to its use: email me at ubacademyofficial@gmail.com. I will reply within one month at the latest.

If you believe your rights are not respected, you can contact the CNDP (Morocco's personal data protection authority) or the data protection authority of your country.
